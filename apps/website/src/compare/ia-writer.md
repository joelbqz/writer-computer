# Writer Computer vs iA Writer

Writer Computer and iA Writer are separate apps from separate makers; the similar names are a coincidence. Both edit plain markdown files on your disk, support `[[wikilinks]]` on the Mac, and render LaTeX math with KaTeX. iA Writer is a paid writing app for Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, built around focused prose: Focus Mode, Style Check, authorship tracking, preview templates and export to PDF and Word. Writer Computer is a free, open-source macOS editor for folders of notes, with Mermaid diagrams, frontmatter properties and one window per workspace.

## Choose iA Writer if

- You write long-form prose and want Focus Mode, Syntax Highlight or Style Check.
- You need iPhone, iPad or Windows apps.
- You export to PDF, Word or HTML, or publish to WordPress, Ghost or Medium.
- You want AI-generated text marked as such (authorship tracking), or content blocks that include other files.

## Choose Writer Computer if

- You want a free, open-source editor. iA Writer for Mac is $49.99 and its source isn't public.
- Your notes use Mermaid diagrams, which iA Writer's docs don't mention, or come from an Obsidian vault or a docs repository.
- You keep several folders open at once, each in its own window.
- A small download matters: about 6 MB for Writer Computer, 17.6 MB for iA Writer from the Mac App Store.

## Moving between them

Both apps work on plain markdown files, so you can open your iA Writer folder in Writer Computer ([how](/docs/open-a-folder/)) and keep writing.

- Wikilinks and `$...$` math carry over.
- iA Writer saves authorship annotations at the end of a file. They are hidden in iA Writer and show as text at the end of the note in other apps, including Writer Computer.
- iA Writer's content blocks and its `{{TOC}}` table of contents aren't supported, and show as plain text in Writer Computer.
